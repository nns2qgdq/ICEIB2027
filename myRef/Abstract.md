本研究旨在將國道車速預測轉化為具明確依據的行前資訊，回應預測準確度未必等同建議有效性的問題。為降低語言模型生成錯誤資訊的風險，提出結合預測工具與官方管制知識庫的可解釋壅塞預測Agent，由程式執行數值計算，語言模型負責意圖解析與說明。系統採用M05A門架資料，聚焦國道5號主線10個區間，預測未來30至60分鐘車速，並設置數字核對及模板回退機制。評估以2024年資料訓練、2025年資料測試，比較持續預測與歷史平均，衡量車速誤差及壅塞偵測能力。初步結果顯示，車速誤差低於持續預測，但連假改善有限，預警能力尚無明顯優勢。後續將檢驗回答正確性與依據可追溯性，以釐清Agent的實際效益。

Abstract— This study aims to translate freeway speed predictions into evidence-based pre-trip information, addressing the gap between predictive accuracy and the effectiveness of travel recommendations. To mitigate the risk of misinformation generated by large language models (LLMs), an explainable congestion prediction agent is proposed that integrates forecasting tools with an official traffic regulation knowledge base. Numerical computations are performed programmatically, while the LLM is responsible for intent interpretation and natural language explanations. The system utilizes M05A gantry traffic data and focuses on ten mainline segments of Taiwan's National Freeway No. 5 to predict traffic speeds 30–60 minutes ahead. Numerical consistency checks and a template-based fallback mechanism are incorporated to improve response reliability. The forecasting models are trained on 2024 data and evaluated on 2025 data against persistence and historical-average baselines, using speed prediction errors and congestion detection performance as evaluation metrics. Preliminary results indicate lower prediction errors than the persistence baseline, although improvements during holiday periods remain limited and no clear advantage in congestion early-warning performance has been observed. Future work will assess response correctness and evidence traceability to determine the practical benefits of the proposed agent.

Index Terms— Congestion prediction, explainable artificial intelligence, intelligent transportation systems, large language models, traffic speed forecasting.
